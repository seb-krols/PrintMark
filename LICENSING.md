# Licensing BaseMark

BaseMark is **free for noncommercial use** and **paid for commercial use**. This page explains
what that means in practice. The legal terms are in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) (the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0/));
if this page and LICENSE.md ever disagree, LICENSE.md wins.

## Who can use it for free

- **Hobbyists and makers** printing for themselves, friends or a club.
- **Student groups and design teams**, such as a university racing or rocketry team.
- **Schools, universities, makerspaces, libraries, charities and public bodies**, including
  their print shops and labs, as long as BaseMark is used for their noncommercial work.
- **Research and teaching**, including studying and changing the code.

## Who needs a paid license

Anyone using BaseMark **to make money**, for example:

- a business printing parts for its own products or production line;
- a print service or print farm that sells prints, even if the labels are only for its own
  sorting;
- a company that bundles BaseMark with a product or offers it as a service.

Not sure? If BaseMark helps you earn revenue, assume it is commercial and ask. Most questions
are answered in one email.

## The monthly limit and license keys

Without a license key, BaseMark stamps up to **100 exports per calendar month** on a PC.
Near the end, the *Stamped* notice counts down the stamps left. After the 100th, exports are
still saved, but **unchanged and without a label**, and the notice says whom to contact.
Stamping starts again on the 1st of the next month.

A **license key** removes the limit. Keys are:

- **free** for noncommercial groups that need more than 100 stamps a month (a busy school
  print shop, a student team before a competition);
- **paid** for commercial use; the key comes with a written commercial license.

Ask for a key at **sebastians.krols@gmail.com** with your organisation, what you print, and
roughly how many exports a month. To enter it, open BaseMark → **Advanced → License → Enter
license key…** (or run `basemark.exe --license-key KEY`). The **Overview** and **Advanced**
pages always show the current state, e.g. *Free use: 37 of 100 stamped exports this month*.

The limit is a reminder, not copy protection: BaseMark's source is readable and the check
runs on your PC. Removing or working around it does not change what the license allows.
Nothing is sent anywhere; the count is a small file (`usage.json`) next to the settings.

## Questions

**We print parts that we then sell. Is that commercial?** Yes, if BaseMark is part of how you
make or sort those parts.

**Our university print shop charges students for material.** Recovering costs for a
noncommercial institution is fine under the license. If you need more than 100 stamps a month,
ask for a free key.

**Can I change the code?** Yes, for noncommercial purposes. Keep the `Required Notice` line
from LICENSE.md in any copy you give to others.

**Can I contribute?** Yes, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). To keep offering
commercial licenses for the whole project, contributions are accepted only with permission
to license them that way too.

Third-party components keep their own licenses; see [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
